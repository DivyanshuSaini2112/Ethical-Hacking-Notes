## Definition

**Social Engineering** in reconnaissance is the manipulation of **people** rather than systems to gather information or gain access.

---

## In-Person Reconnaissance Techniques

### 1. Dumpster Diving
**Definition:** Physically searching through an organization's trash/recycling to find valuable information.

**What can be found:**
- Printed documents with passwords
- Company memos and org charts
- Discarded hardware with data
- Old badges/keycards

**Example:** Finding shredded documents that reveal internal project names or employee structures.

---

### 2. Shoulder Surfing
**Definition:** Physically observing someone as they enter sensitive information (passwords, PINs, etc.).

**Contexts:**
- ATMs — watching PIN entry
- Offices — observing login credentials
- Coffee shops — watching someone work on confidential docs

**Example:** Standing near an employee in a coffee shop and watching them type their VPN password.

---

### 3. Tailgating
**Definition:** Following an authorized person through a secure door/entrance without their permission.

**Also called:** Piggybacking

**Example:** Waiting near a key-card entry door, then following an employee through without swiping your own card.

---

### 4. Social Engineering (Broad)
**Definition:** Manipulating people psychologically to reveal confidential information.

**Types:**
- **Phishing** — deceptive emails
- **Vishing** — voice/phone deception
- **Pretexting** — creating a fabricated scenario
- **Baiting** — leaving infected USB drives

**Example:** Calling IT help desk pretending to be an employee who forgot their password.

---

## Summary Table

| Technique | Physical/Digital | Risk Level |
|---|---|---|
| Dumpster Diving | Physical | Medium |
| Shoulder Surfing | Physical | Low |
| Tailgating | Physical | High |
| Social Engineering | Both | Very High |

---

## Countermeasures

1. **Shred all sensitive documents** before disposal
2. **Privacy screens** on monitors in public areas
3. **Security awareness training** — teach employees to spot social engineering
4. **Strict access control** — challenge unfamiliar faces at entry points
5. **Clean desk policy** — no sensitive info left unattended
6. **Visitor management system** — log and escort all visitors
