# 🔐 Ethical Hacking - Definition and Overview

> **MOC:** [[MOC - Ethical Hacking Fundamentals]] | **Tags:** `#ethical-hacking` `#fundamentals`

---

## Definition

**Ethical Hacking** is the **authorized and legal practice** of identifying security vulnerabilities in computer systems, networks, and applications by simulating attacks performed by malicious hackers.

> Ethical Hackers use the **same tools and techniques** as attackers, but with **prior permission** and for the purpose of improving security systems.

---

## Key Characteristics

- ✅ **Legal & Authorized** — requires written permission
- ✅ **Same tools** as malicious hackers — but used ethically
- ✅ **Goal:** identify & fix security weaknesses *before* malicious attackers exploit them
- ✅ Uses **real hacking techniques** with permission to strengthen cybersecurity
- ✅ Protects **digital assets**

---

## Why Ethical Hacking Exists

| Problem | Ethical Hacking Solution |
|---|---|
| Unknown vulnerabilities | Simulate real attacks to find them |
| Weak security posture | Assess and strengthen defenses |
| Compliance requirements | Meet legal/regulatory standards |
| Financial & reputational loss | Prevent breaches proactively |

---

## Core Benefits

1. **Identifies vulnerabilities** before attackers exploit them
2. **Protects sensitive data**
3. **Evaluates effectiveness** of existing security controls
4. **Compliance** with legal and regulatory requirements
5. **Reduces financial and reputational loss**
6. **Business continuity and trust**

---

## Ethical Hacking vs Malicious Hacking

| Parameter | Ethical Hacking | Malicious Hacking |
|---|---|---|
| Legality | Legal & Authorized | Illegal & Unauthorized |
| Permission | Performed **with** permission | Performed **without** permission |
| Aim | Improve security | Cause harm or gain profit |
| Ethics | Follows ethical guidelines | Violates laws & ethics |
| Reporting | Reports vulnerabilities responsibly | Exploits vulnerabilities secretly |
