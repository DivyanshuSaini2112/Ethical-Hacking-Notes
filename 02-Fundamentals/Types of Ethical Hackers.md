## The Hat Color System

Hackers are categorized by the **color of their "hat"** — a metaphor for their intent and authorization level.

---

## 🤍 White Hat Hacker

- **Definition:** Ethical security professional who hacks with full authorization
- **Also called:** Ethical Hacker, Penetration Tester
- **Works for:** Organizations, security firms
- **Goal:** Find and fix vulnerabilities **before** attackers do
- **Example:** A company hires a pen tester to audit their network

---

## 🖤 Black Hat Hacker

- **Definition:** Malicious hacker who operates **without authorization**
- **Also called:** Cracker
- **Goal:** Cause damage, steal data, gain financial profit
- **Legal status:** Criminal — violates laws in all jurisdictions
- **Example:** Hackers who break into banks to steal funds

---

## 🩶 Grey Hat Hacker

- **Definition:** Operates **between** white and black hat
- **Behavior:** May hack without permission but discloses vulnerabilities
- **Motive:** Not always malicious, but still unauthorized
- **Example:** Hacker finds a flaw in a website and reports it publicly without asking permission first

---

## 🔵 Blue Hat Hacker

- **Definition:** Security professional hired **from outside** an organization to test a product before launch
- **Context:** Often used in software/product security testing
- **Example:** Microsoft invites external testers for Windows before release

---

## 🟢 Green Hat Hacker

- **Definition:** A **beginner/newbie** hacker who is eager to learn
- **Characteristic:** Learning the craft; not yet skilled enough to be dangerous
- **Example:** Students learning hacking in ethical hacking courses

---

## 🔴 Red Hat Hacker (Eagle-Eyed Hacker)

- **Definition:** Aggressive defenders who **attack black hat hackers** directly
- **Approach:** Instead of reporting, they **destroy** the attacker's system
- **Also called:** Vigilante hackers
- **Behavior:** Upload viruses, DoS attacks on black hats

---

## Summary Table

| Hat Color | Authorization | Intent | Legal |
|---|---|---|---|
| White 🤍 | Authorized | Defensive | ✅ Legal |
| Black 🖤 | Unauthorized | Malicious | ❌ Illegal |
| Grey 🩶 | Often unauthorized | Mixed | ⚠️ Borderline |
| Blue 🔵 | Authorized (external) | Defensive | ✅ Legal |

| Red 🔴 | Self-authorized | Aggressive defense | ⚠️ Borderline |
