# 🛡️ Cyberethics - Core Principles

> **MOC:** [[MOC - Cyber Law]] | **Tags:** `#cyberethics` `#ethics`

---

## Definition

**Cyberethics** is a branch of computer technology that defines the **best practices** that must be adopted by a user when using a computer system.

In simple terms: **the study of ethical issues related to the use of technology.**

**It:**
- Engages users to use the internet **safely** and **responsibly**
- Addresses **privacy, security, digital rights, and responsible use**
- Ensures individuals and organizations act in ways that **respect both legal and moral guidelines** in the digital world

---

## 6 Core Principles of Cyberethics

### 1. 🔒 Privacy
Ensures personal data and information are kept **secure and protected** from unauthorized access or misuse.

> Individuals' privacy rights must be respected in the digital world.

### 2. ✅ Integrity
Promoting **honesty and accuracy** in digital communications, transactions, and content.

- Avoid creation or spread of **false information**
- Ensure data is **not altered without authorization**

### 3. 👤 Accountability
Individuals and organizations should be **held accountable** for their actions in the digital space.

- Taking **responsibility** for digital content, behavior, and consequences of online actions

### 4. 🛡️ Security
Protecting systems, networks, and data from **cyberattacks, unauthorized access, and vulnerabilities**.

- Cybersecurity measures must be implemented to **safeguard the digital environment**

### 5. 🌐 Access
Ensuring **equal access** to digital resources and technology for everyone, regardless of:
- Socioeconomic status
- Geographic location
- Other barriers

> The digital divide should be minimized to promote **inclusivity**.

### 6. 💻 Digital Responsibility
Encouraging **ethical behavior** in the use of technology:
- Respecting others' intellectual property rights
- Avoiding cyberbullying
- Ensuring responsible use of online platforms and tools

## Breaches of Cyberethics (Cyber Crimes)

| # | Breach | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Man in the Middle** | Intercepting communications between two parties |
| 02 | **Drive-By Downloads** | Malware installed when visiting a compromised site |
| 03 | **Malvertising** | Malicious code embedded in advertisements |
| 04 | **Rogue Software** | Fake security software that is actually malware |
| 05 | **DDoS** | Overwhelming systems with traffic to deny service |
| 06 | **Password Attacks** | Brute force, dictionary attacks on credentials |
| 07 | **Phishing** | Deceptive messages to steal credentials |
| 08 | **Malware** | Viruses, worms, ransomware, spyware |

---

## Ethical Hacking vs Cyberethics

| Ethical Hacking | Cyberethics Principle Respected |
|---|---|
| Performed with authorization | Accountability |
| Protects systems | Security |
| Discloses findings responsibly | Integrity |
| Doesn't share client data | Privacy |
| Operates within legal scope | Digital Responsibility |
