# 💡 Intellectual Property in Cyberspace

> **MOC:** [[MOC - Cyber Law]] | **Tags:** `#intellectual-property` `#copyright` `#cyber-law`

---

## Definition

**Intellectual Property (IP)** refers to the **creation of the mind** — assets that may or may not be physical such as literature, music, artistic works, ideas, symbols, logos, etc.

In the context of **cyberspace**: digital assets that originally belong to an individual/organization that **cannot be used by anyone without their consent** and are legally protected if violated.

---

## Categories of Intellectual Property Law

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| **Copyrights** | Protects original creative works |
| **Trademark** | Protects brand identifiers (logos, names) |
| **Trade Secrets** | Protects confidential business information |
| **Patent** | Protects inventions |
| **Industrial Designs** | Protects aesthetic design of products |
| **Geographical Indications** | Protects location-based product origins |

---

## IP in Cyberspace - Why It Matters

- With the expansion of technology, cyberspace is a major **business platform** → increasing pressure on IP
- Cyber crimes now include **copyright and trademark infringement**
- Online content needs to be protected → **IPR and Cyber laws cannot be separated**

> In cyberspace, a person may make profit by using another's creation without consent → violation of IPR

---

## Two Major IP Issues in Cyberspace

### 1. 📄 Copyright Infringement in Cyberspace

**Copyright** protects the owner of any **published artistic, literary, or scientific work** from others exploiting it in their name.

When proprietary creations are used **without the owner's permission** → **copyright infringement**.

**Most common cases of copyright infringement:**

#### A) Linking
- Permits a website user to visit another location on the internet
- **Damages:** Creates the supposition that two linked sites promote the same idea → linked sites lose income (based on page visits)

#### B) Software Piracy
Stealing software that is lawfully protected — copying, spreading, altering, or trading software.

Also covered under the **Indian Copyright Act**.

**3 Types of Software Piracy:**

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Soft Lifting** | Installing software on MORE computers than the license allows. E.g., one license installed on multiple machines. |
| **Software Counterfeiting** | Illegal reproduction/distribution/sale of software made to look like the original — sold as genuine. |
| **Uploading-Downloading** | Illegal exchange of copyrighted content, software, or data online — leads to piracy, data breaches, IP theft. |

---

### 2. ™️ Trademark Issues in Cyberspace

**Trademark** = a mark capable of being depicted visually that may distinguish products/services of one person from others.

**Trademark Infringement** = **unlawful use of a trademark or service mark** which causes ambiguity, fraud, or confusion about the actual company.

**Common trademark issues online:**

#### A) Cybersquatting
**Unauthorized registration and use** of internet domain names similar to business trademarks/service marks — to make money off a well-known brand.

> Example: 'Xyze' is a famous company. A cybersquatter buys 'xyz.com' to confuse users and take advantage of brand equity.

**Domain Name Dispute:** When more than one individual claims rights to register a specific domain name — arises when a registered trademark is registered by another non-owner.

#### B) Branding on Websites
Using a trademark without permission on **websites, social media profiles, or online marketplaces** — causing confusion about origin of goods/services.

## Copyright Laws in Cyberspace

| Law | Year | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **DMCA** (Digital Millennium Copyright Act) | 1998 (USA) | Criminalizes unauthorized distribution online; "safe harbor" for ISPs |
| **WIPO Copyright Treaty (WCT)** | 1996 (Global) | Protects authors' rights in digital environment globally |
| **EU Copyright Directive** | 2001/29/EC | Harmonizes copyright across EU; platform responsibility |

### DMCA Details
- Combats copyright infringement on the internet
- Criminalizes **unauthorized distribution of copyrighted material** (digital works)
- Provides **"safe harbor"** for online service providers if they remove infringing content when notified
- Covers: **text, images, videos, software, and music** shared online
- Copyright holders can send **take-down notices** to hosting platforms

### WIPO Copyright Treaty (WCT)
- Ensures protection of authors' rights in **digital and internet-based media**
- Provides copyright holders **exclusive rights to control how works are used online**
- Ensures protection of **digital content globally**

## Trademark Laws in Cyberspace

| Law | Jurisdiction | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Lanham Act (1946)** | USA | Federal trademark protection in commerce |
| **UDRP** (Uniform Domain Name Dispute Resolution Policy) | Global (ICANN) | Resolve domain name disputes infringing trademarks |
| **EU Trademark Directive** | EU | Online brand protection, prevent unauthorized trademark use |

### UDRP
- Administered by **ICANN**
- Quick, cost-effective alternative to litigation for domain disputes
- Helps businesses protect brand from misuse via similar domain names

### Lanham Act
- Helps prevent use of domain names or social media handles that infringe trademarks
- Addresses cybersquatting

## Key Points Summary

- Copyrights and trademarks now extend to **intellectual property rights over the internet**
- Cyberspace is becoming a **hub for IPR infringement**
- Indian **IT Act does NOT cover** cybercrimes related to IPR, cyberstalking, cyber defamation
- **Indian Trademark Act 1999** and **Copyright Act 1957** are **silent on online infringement**
- Computer programs ARE protected under Copyright Act 1957, but **no remedies for cyberpiracy**
